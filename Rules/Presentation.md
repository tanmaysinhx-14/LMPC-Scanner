## Proposed Solution

* **Detailed Explanation:** Describe your core idea, solution, or prototype clearly and concisely.
* **Problem Resolution:** Explain exactly how this solution addresses the identified problem.
* **Innovation & Uniqueness:** Highlight what sets your solution apart from existing alternatives.

---

## Technical Approach

* **Technology Stack:** List specific programming languages, frameworks, and hardware to be utilized.
* **Implementation Methodology:** Outline the step-by-step process, incorporating flowcharts, architectural diagrams, or a working prototype.

---

## Feasibility and Viability

* **Feasibility Analysis:** Evaluate the practical and technical execution of the idea given current resources.
* **Potential Challenges:** Identify anticipated risks, bottlenecks, and technical hurdles.
* **Mitigation Strategies:** Provide concrete plans and fallback options for overcoming these identified challenges.

---

## Impact and Benefits

* **Target Audience Impact:** Describe how the solution directly improves the situation for the end-users.
* **Broader Benefits:** Outline the quantifiable social, economic, or environmental advantages of the project.

---

## Research and References

* **Citations:** Provide structured details and accessible links to all supporting research work and references.
